## 1. Executive Inventory Summary

Every visual asset across the DHEERA e-commerce storefront, administrative control panels, and transactional customer touchpoints has been catalogued, measured, and verified. 

The image architecture adheres to strict luxury e-commerce proportion standards:
- **Master Garment Portrait Standard:** Exact 3:4 aspect ratio (`1200 × 1600 px` master, scaled dynamically down to `48px` thumbnails via Next.js Image Optimization).
- **Comprehensive 6-Slot Multi-Angle Gallery:** Every garment provisions 6 distinct views: Front (Cover), Back, Detail (Embroidery/Neckline), Border (Hemline/Sleeve), Fabric (Macro Weave), and Styling (Editorial Context).
- **Hero / Editorial Banner Standard:** 16:9 full HD (`1920 × 1080 px`) landscape proportion.
- **Category Banner Standard:** 2:1 widescreen (`1200 × 600 px`) landscape proportion.
- **Brand Identity Standard:** 1:1 square master (`2000 × 2000 px` crisp PNG).
