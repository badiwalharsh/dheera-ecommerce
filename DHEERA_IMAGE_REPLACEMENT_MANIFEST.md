## 1. Replacement Overview & Policy Compliance

This manifest specifies the complete before-and-after mapping for all visual assets across the DHEERA e-commerce storefront.

### Strict Policy Enforcements
1. **Zero Collages:** No collage layouts, multiple embedded frames, or multi-garment composite tiles exist in any product slot. Each image is a single, standalone high-resolution asset.
2. **Authentic 6-Slot Multi-Angle Specification:** Every prototype garment provisions:
   - `front`: Full-length front silhouette (Cover Image)
   - `back`: Full-length back silhouette and drape
   - `detail`: High-resolution embroidery and neckline craftsmanship
   - `border`: High-resolution hemline, sleeve cuff, or border trim
   - `fabric`: Macro texture inspection of fabric weave and thread count
   - `styling`: Lifestyle / editorial styling context
3. **Internal Concept Labeling:** AI-generated illustrative imagery is explicitly documented as concept imagery for development, storefront testing, and prototyping. It is never misrepresented to customers or commercial partners as photographs of physical stock or authenticated fabric swatches.
4. **Reversible Preservation:** Original early-phase low-resolution assets are non-destructively preserved in `public/images/products/originals/` and `public/images/banners/originals/`.
