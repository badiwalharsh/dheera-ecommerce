# DHEERA — Product Photography & Visual Asset Guide

**Brand:** DHEERA — Apparel & Lifestyle  
**Descriptor:** Indian Heritage, Contemporary Soul  
**Tagline:** Made in India | For the World.  
**Version:** 1.0 (Phase 8.5 Commercial Readiness)  

---

## 1. Overview & Luxury Standards

DHEERA’s digital storefront is built to evoke the understated grandeur of Indian luxury apparel. The website uses strict **3:4 portrait aspect ratio containers** across all product cards, quick views, galleries, carts, and checkout surfaces.

To present garments with authenticity and high conversion appeal:
1. **Never upscale low-resolution smartphone snapshots.** High-resolution camera sensors (minimum 24 MP) are recommended.
2. **Never apply artificial textures or synthetic generative filters** to represent artisan embroidery. True craftsmanship must be visible in the raw threadwork.
3. **Capture color fidelity faithfully.** Indian festive shades (Rani Pink, Sindoor Red, Emerald, Mustard, Jamuni, Warm Ivory) must match real fabric dyes under standardized color profiles (sRGB).

## 2. Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspect Ratio** | **3:4 Portrait** (Strict) | All storefront containers enforce `aspect-[3/4]`. |
| **Recommended Dimensions** | **2400 × 3200 px** | Minimum acceptable: **2000 × 2500 px**. |
| **Maximum File Size** | **10 MB per image** | Compressed via WebP / Progressive JPEG. |
| **Accepted Formats** | **JPEG (.jpg), PNG (.png), WebP (.webp)** | WebP is automatically generated by Next.js CDN. |
| **Color Space** | **sRGB** | Avoid AdobeRGB or CMYK to prevent dull colors on mobile. |
| **Background Tone** | **Neutral Warm Cream / Pale Ivory** | Hex `#FAF9F5` or `#EEEAE0`; soft shadow, no harsh cutouts. |
| **Lighting** | **Soft diffused daylight or continuous 5500K studio strobe** | No direct flash bounce; preserve zari luster without hot spots. |

## 3. The 6 Mandatory Angles Per Garment

To ensure customer confidence and reduce returns, every published garment should feature a minimum of **4 to 6 authentic photographic angles**:

### Shot 1: Hero Front Full-Length View (Primary Cover Image)
- **Framing:** Full ensemble from head/collar to hemline/feet.
- **Purpose:** Primary catalogue thumbnail on homepage, `/shop`, and category collections.
- **Pose:** Natural standing posture facing the camera, hands relaxed or lightly holding the dupatta drape.

### Shot 2: Hero Back Full-Length View
- **Framing:** Complete back silhouette.
- **Purpose:** Displays back yoke embroidery, back neckline depth, dori tassels, and trouser drape.

### Shot 3: Mid-Torso / Neckline Detail (The Craftsmanship Shot)
- **Framing:** Tight bust-to-waist crop (horizontal width approx. 50–60 cm of garment).
- **Purpose:** Focuses on neckline embellishments: zardozi, handblock prints, tilla work, gota patti, or button details.

### Shot 4: Macro Fabric Weave & Embroidery Texture
- **Framing:** 1:1 macro close-up (approx. 15–20 cm of fabric).
- **Purpose:** Allows customers using the website's **2x Texture Zoom** to inspect genuine silk grain, slub cotton weave, and metallic zari sheen.

### Shot 5: Hemline, Border & Trousers / Saree Pallu View
- **Framing:** Lower third of kurta, saree pallu fall, or trousers ankle hem.
- **Purpose:** Displays scalloped embroidery, border lace, side slits, and bottom tailoring.

### Shot 6: Editorial Styling / Movement Shot (Optional but Recommended)
- **Framing:** Three-quarter angle in gentle motion (walking, turning, natural drape flow).
- **Purpose:** Communicates garment fluidity, flare diameter (for anarkalis and lehengas), and breathability.

## 4. Category-Specific Guidelines

### Kurta Sets
- Ensure matching trousers/pants are visible in at least one full shot.
- Show dupatta draped over one shoulder and open in another shot.
- Feature pocket detail if the garment includes side pockets.

### Sarees
- Shot 1 must showcase the pleated front and shoulder pallu drape clearly.
- Shot 2 must showcase the complete pallu pattern and border contrast.
- Include a flat-lay or folded view displaying the unstitched blouse piece fabric provided.

### Suits & Anarkalis
- Show the complete flare diameter (ghera).
- Capture wrist/cuff detailing on full-sleeve ensembles.

### Dupattas
- Display the drape fully opened to show border width and corner tassels.
- Highlight translucent sheer fabric (organza/chanderi) against soft light.

## 5. File Preparation & Naming Convention

Before uploading to the DHEERA Admin Portal, name files systematically by SKU:

```
[SKU]_[ANGLE_INDEX]_[DESCRIPTION].[ext]
```

### Examples:
- `DHR-KRT-101_01_front.jpg` *(Main Cover Image)*
- `DHR-KRT-101_02_back.jpg`
- `DHR-KRT-101_03_neckline.jpg`
- `DHR-KRT-101_04_macro_weave.jpg`
- `DHR-KRT-101_05_hemline.jpg`
- `DHR-KRT-101_06_editorial.jpg`

## 6. How to Upload in Admin Dashboard

1. Navigate to `/admin/products`.
2. Click **Create New Garment** (or Edit an existing draft).
3. Scroll to the **Garment Photography & Visual Assets** section.
4. Drag and drop high-resolution JPG/PNG/WebP files (up to 10 MB each).
5. Designate the first image as **Cover Image** (displays as catalogue thumbnail).
6. Reorder images using **Move Left / Move Right** buttons to match the recommended sequence.
7. Enter descriptive **Image Alt Text** (e.g. *"Rani pink embroidered kurta set with zari border"*).
8. Use the **Mobile (390px) & Desktop Preview Toggle** to verify thumbnail presentation before publishing.

---

## 7. Storage Security Boundary Note

- All product images uploaded via the product management workflow are stored in the public `products` bucket (`CANONICAL_PUBLIC_BUCKET`) and distributed via high-performance CDN.
- Private customer measurements, body scans, and bespoke design references uploaded on `/product/custom` are stored strictly in the private `customizations` bucket (`CANONICAL_PRIVATE_BUCKET`) and are accessible only by authenticated staff and the owning customer.
