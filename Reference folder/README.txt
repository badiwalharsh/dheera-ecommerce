DHEERA storefront — visual catalogue preview (version 3)

Open index.html in a modern browser. The assets folder contains generated, DHEERA-inspired editorial fashion images used in the hero, collection tiles and six product cards.

Included:
- Responsive minimal-luxury storefront in DHEERA's ivory and forest-green palette
- Brand logo, editorial hero, collection tiles and six matching product photos
- Product search, category filters and price sorting
- Product details, size selection, shopping bag and local cart persistence
- Customisation enquiry link to DHEERA's Instagram

Important before launch:
- All six product names, descriptions and prices are illustrative placeholders, not confirmed inventory or offers.
- The generated fashion images are concept imagery, not photographs of actual DHEERA stock. Replace them with real product photos before taking orders.
- Checkout, payment processing, inventory, customer accounts, order management, email and shipping are not connected.
- A production launch requires a secure backend/database, admin product tools, payment gateway, shipping/tax configuration, customer notifications, privacy/terms/returns policies, and hosting/domain setup.
